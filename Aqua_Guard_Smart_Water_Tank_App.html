About Our Project

Project name: Smart Water Tank Alert System

This project aims to prevent water wastage in schools and apartments by monitoring the amount of water in a storage tank. A sensor measures the water level, a microcontroller processes the reading, and an alert informs the caretaker when the tank is almost full or the water level is low.

How the System Works

  1. Sense: The ultrasonic sensor measures the distance to the water surface.
  2. Decide: The microcontroller compares the reading with preset water-level limits.
  3. Alert: A buzzer or indicator provides a warning.
  4. Respond: The caretaker checks the tank and takes suitable action.

Main Benefits

  • Helps prevent tank overflow.
  • Reduces unnecessary water wastage.
  • Alerts caretakers when water is low.
  • Encourages responsible water management.
  • Can be expanded with mobile notifications and automatic pump control.
